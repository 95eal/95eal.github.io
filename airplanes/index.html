Hide your planes. Hunt theirs.

One headshot brings a plane down. Play solo, on your local network or online.

How it plays

  1. 1

    Hide your three planes

    Place them on your grid before the match.

  2. 2

    A hit shows you where it is

    Turns alternate. Only a headshot brings a plane down, so read the board and close in.

  3. 3

    Three headshots win the match

    The first to bring down every enemy plane wins.
